Side finding — correctness (not Big O)

RoomSegmenter.contours_to_polygons (room_segmenter.py:81) silently drops invalid contours. Its callers still index the result as if it lined up with contours:

  • floor_plan_extractor.py:219 uses label_matches.get(i) with a polygon index, but label_matches is keyed by contour index.
  • sanborn_parser.py:141 uses zip(polygons, contours).

After the first dropped contour, every later room gets the wrong label and material. Fix: have contours_to_polygons return (contour_index, polygon) pairs.

Method & limits

  • Coverage: I ran bigo-lint 1.x over backend/, frontend/src, frontend/e2e and scripts/, and enumerated every construct through its engine API to get the totals.
  • Unannotated code: I screened every outermost unannotated loop or iteration call for nesting or linear-cost calls inside the loop (indexOf/includes/find/filter, sort, DB/HTTP calls, in <list>). That gave 36 candidates, which I read by hand: 10 confirmed defects, 26 linear or bounded.
  • Not covered: super-linear cost reached through a call to an unannotated helper from a plain function body (no loop at the call site), and costs inside SQL (query plans were not EXPLAINed). The JS/TS parser is heuristic (see /usr/local/lib/bigo-lint/README.html). The 798 unannotated constructs were not individually given bounds. Doing that would be the full retrofit, which is deferred by your 2026-10-02 decision.
  • Linter report: bigo_report.html (repo root) lists the 9 annotated super-linear constructs and their exceptions.
